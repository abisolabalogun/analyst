About me

From the ward to the dashboard

I am Abisola, a healthcare professional based in Cheltenham. I have worked in hospitals, care homes, live-in care, supported living and agency settings in Nigeria and the UK. In those jobs, a missed detail can affect a real person.

That is the standard I bring to data. I am retraining as a data analyst through the 10Alytics HealthTech Business Analysis programme, and I build projects that answer real questions: why patients miss appointments, why customers leave, and where fraud slips through.

I understand both sides, the people who give care and the people who need the numbers to make decisions.

My process

How I work on a project

  1. Understand

    I start with the question and who needs the answer, before touching any data.

  2. Clean and explore

    I check the data for errors and gaps, then look for patterns.

  3. Visualise

    I build charts and dashboards that make the finding obvious.

  4. Recommend

    I finish with clear next steps, in plain English.
