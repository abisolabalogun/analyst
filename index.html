About me

Clinical insight meets business analysis

I am Abisola, a healthtech business analyst based in Cheltenham, UK. I have spent over five years in hospitals, care homes, live-in care, supported living and agency settings across Nigeria and the UK, so I know how healthcare services really run and where they lose time, information and trust.

I use that understanding to gather requirements, manage delivery and analyse data with SQL, Excel and Power BI. The result is analysis that is accurate, easy to read and tied to a decision.

I work well with clinicians, managers and technical teams, and I am comfortable handling sensitive information with care.
